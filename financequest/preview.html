How mascot growth works

Prototype rules

This is one customizable mascot. Its growth stage is tied to the total percentage saved toward all goals, while budget XP rewards daily responsible-spending check-ins. Spending money does not instantly shrink your mascot. This gives your team a simple, positive approach to gamification to demonstrate to your professor.
